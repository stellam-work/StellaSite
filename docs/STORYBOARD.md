# Full Portfolio Storyboard

## Motion thesis

Stella progresses through six stations on a single bottom stage. The opening 22% of each incoming chapter advances to its station; the remaining scroll performs that chapter's gesture. Forward travel uses the authored right-walk cycle, while reverse travel unwinds the gesture before using the independent left-walk cycle. The feet remain on one baseline throughout.

## Entry - Shoreline

- Purpose: establish identity before the technical narrative.
- Character: no overlay; the supplied shoreline poster/video remains dominant.
- Exit: the bottom stage appears after the hero and starts at the left edge.
- Mobile: portrait media; reduced motion uses the poster only.

## 01 - Systems

- Purpose: manual fragments become a reliable operating pipeline.
- Gesture: frames 045-054 gradually raise and extend an upward-right point, hold, then return toward neutral.
- Placement: global bottom rail; no vertical movement.
- Background: scattered slats align into a pipeline and resolve into signal nodes.
- Reduced motion: frame 054.

## 02 - Scale

- Purpose: high-volume data condenses into precise processing and monitoring.
- Gesture: frames 035-044 raise an upward-left point toward the reading column, hold, then return.
- Placement: continues rightward along the bottom rail.
- Background: distributed data blocks condense toward the processing node.
- Reduced motion: frame 044.

## 03 - Stable Diffusion

- Purpose: frame recursive synthetic training as an investigation rather than a decorative loop.
- Gesture: frames 062-068 move incrementally from neutral attention to hand-on-chin discovery.
- Placement: bottom rail, fixed foot baseline.
- Background: three generation beds change emphasis while the thesis link remains semantic HTML.
- Reduced motion: frame 068.

## 04 - Transfer

- Purpose: show one method adapting across legal data, biochemical research, and constraint programming.
- Gesture: frames 055-061 open the palm and present the changing domain field.
- Placement: bottom rail, with walk direction determined by scroll direction.
- Background: three distinct procedural arrangements correspond to the three domain blocks.
- Reduced motion: frame 061.

## 05 - Leadership

- Purpose: make coordination feel like a human exchange rather than an orbit or mechanical alignment.
- Gesture: frames 055-061 open into a calm presentation, hold, and return toward neutral.
- Background: contributions enter sequentially from alternating sides, settle into a rising shared path, connect, and light the next handoff in a call-and-response rhythm.
- Header relationship: the open palm arrives after the heading settles; the final handoff lands after both leadership roles have reading time.
- Placement: bottom rail with no lift, crossing, bow, or abrupt pose swap.
- Reduced motion: frame 061.

## 06 - Contact

- Purpose: end with an open, positive invitation.
- Gesture: frames 069-075 build incrementally from recognition to a restrained delighted response.
- Placement: reaches the right end of the bottom rail.
- Background: the final magenta signal line stays simple so email and evidence actions remain clear.
- Reduced motion: frame 075.

## Continuity and fallback

- Section and global progress are recomputed on load, resize, rotation, anchor jumps, and reverse scroll.
- Forward and reverse travel have separately authored walk cycles; sprites are never mirrored.
- WebGL failure uses the same chapter stills on the CSS bottom rail.
- Reduced motion removes walking and displays one meaningful static pose per chapter.
